# apps/owner — the owner app. Austere by law. (spec §14.2)

Every proposed feature must **reduce** the principal's cognitive load or it
is cut. The owner buys silence; the console absorbs the noise (§14.4).

React Native, M5. Exactly four surfaces:

1. **One status glyph.** All properties secure — where green means
   *verified* green (log chain intact, devices healthy, posture applied),
   not "no alerts yet".
2. **Daily briefing.** One paragraph, agent-written from the read-only tools,
   every claim traceable to event ids.
3. **Approval requests** — the only interruption. "Operator requests
   ANNOUNCE at the north fence. **Approve / Deny / Call me.**" Approval on
   the phone produces the second key of the two-key model exactly as
   hold-to-authorize does on the console.
4. **People & access; Panic; Lockdown.** Grant, revoke, expiring guest
   passes. Panic and Lockdown are DENY-rung passive effects and require no
   grant to *stop* anything — the safe verb needs no key.

Also binding here (§15.6): every access to the family's data — including by
our own staff — is an audit record **the owner can read in this app**. Data
sovereignty is the sale.

`ApprovalRequest.tsx` sketches surface 3; the rest land with M5.
